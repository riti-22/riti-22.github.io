01 / About

Engineer by training. Curious by nature.

I enjoy working where hardware, software, perception, and human experience meet.

I studied Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering at Nirma University, with a minor in Data Science. My work and projects have taken me across embedded systems, computer vision, machine learning, and robotics.

I'm especially interested in socially assistive robots and human–robot interaction: building systems that are not only technically capable, but also intuitive and meaningful to interact with.

I like moving between disciplines, learning by building, and asking how intelligent systems can fit into the varied ways people live and communicate.
