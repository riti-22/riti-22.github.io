Computer vision, reinforcement learning

An autonomous tennis-ball-collecting robot

Built as an affordable alternative to imported systems for tennis academies. The platform moved from a laser-cut acrylic prototype to a 600 × 500 mm welded-steel frame, with an ESP32 for embedded control and vision running on a Raspberry Pi 5.

After Docker and Hailo accelerator problems blocked a ROS 2 pipeline, I moved to PyBullet and then NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and trained a PPO navigation agent across 16 parallel environments. Reward shaping fixed an agent that spun in circles and taught it to navigate around the net. A YOLO model trained on 4,935 court images replaced a hand-tuned colour filter for net detection.

The trained policy has not yet run on the physical robot. Sim-to-real transfer is the next step.

An emotion-aware humanoid robot

A real-time pipeline transcribes speech, classifies it into 11 emotions with a DistilBERT model fine-tuned on GoEmotions, and responds with a gesture and a spoken reply on a Hiwonder TonyPi Pro humanoid driven by a Raspberry Pi 5.

Presented to the National Board of Accreditation committee during my university’s institutional review. A research manuscript using EmoBot as a case study is in preparation.
